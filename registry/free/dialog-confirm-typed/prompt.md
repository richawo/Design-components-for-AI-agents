Build a destructive confirmation dialog in React 19 with Tailwind v4 and motion/react. It is a danger-zone card with one trigger button, and pressing it makes a panel grow out of that button and cover the card under a scrim. The visitor must type the exact resource name before the red Delete button enables, so an accidental Enter never deletes anything.

**Layout.** The root is `@container`, centred and transparent, with 40px of vertical padding (48px from `sm`), and is sized by the card alone: the dialog never reserves room in the layout. The demo passes a rounded, hairline-framed settings pane through `className`. The card is up to 560px wide, 16px radius, 20px padding (24px from `@md`). Inside it: a mono kicker, a Geist display title, a muted sentence, and the trigger at the end of the row (stacked under the text below `@md`). The scrim and the panel are `position: fixed`: the scrim covers the viewport (z-index 60) and the panel is centred in it (z-index 61), `min(460px, 100vw - 2rem)` wide and at most `100dvh - 2rem` tall with `overflow-y: auto`, so the dialog is reachable from any screen wherever the card sits, even a 390×560 landscape phone with the card at the bottom. 18px radius, 24px padding (28px from 480px wide and 641px tall). At 640px tall or less the padding drops to 20px and the ledger, field and footer spacing tightens by 4px so the whole dialog still fits a 560px viewport; if anything still overflows, it scrolls inside the panel and the focused field is scrolled into view. The panel content runs top to bottom: kicker “Permanent deletion” (which becomes the error line; both lines sit invisibly in one grid cell so a wrapped error moves nothing), title “Delete {name}” as an h2, a body sentence, a ledger of what goes (an array prop, default Environments 12, Last deploy 3 h ago, Environment variables 47; the figure is mono, and a suffix such as “h ago” is body sans in the muted colour, joined by a thin space) separated by hairlines, the confirmation field, and a footer. The footer is designed per panel width (the panel is its own `@container`), with Cancel and Delete first in the DOM so Tab goes from the field straight to them. From 400px of content (`@[25rem]`) it is one row: “Archive instead” on the left (pulled 12px left so its text aligns with the content edge) and Cancel plus Delete (min-width 168px) on the right. Below that, from 280px (`@[17.5rem]`), Cancel and Delete share a full-width row with Delete filling the rest, and Archive instead sits centred under them. Narrower (a 320px phone), Delete stacks over a full-width Cancel. The trigger and every control are 44px tall. Nothing ever wraps by accident.

**Typography.** Title: display face, 24px, line-height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.03em. Body: 14px, line-height 1.55. Kickers: mono, 10.5px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, muted. Ledger labels 13.5px body colour; figures mono 13.5px with tabular numerals. The confirmation label is 13px and the resource name inside it is mono. The field is mono 14px.

**Colour.** Dark: card #121214, panel #161618, field #0e0e10, hairline rgba(255,255,255,0.08), rule rgba(255,255,255,0.07), ink #f4f4f5, body #b4b4bc, muted #8a8a93, scrim rgba(0,0,0,0.72), success #5cb86f. Light: card and panel #ffffff, field #f6f6f7, hairline rgba(24,24,27,0.10), ink #18181b, body #3f3f46, muted #6b6b74, scrim rgba(18,18,20,0.5), success #2f7d43. The tone sets one fill, one ink and one wash. Danger: fill #d63b40 (hover #e2484d) with white text, ink #ff8b8f on dark; fill #dc2626 (hover #c51f1f), ink #b91c1c on light. Neutral: fill #e9e9ec (hover #ffffff) with #0b0b0c text on dark, #18181b (hover #2e2e33) with white text on light. Only an enabled, idle Delete takes its hover step. Colour is used only for the destructive action and its trigger; nothing else is tinted. There is no warning triangle.

**Motion.** Open: the panel grows out of the trigger as a disc. Behind the panel sits a fixed, rounded (18px), `overflow: hidden` frame the size of the panel’s resting box, holding a disc in the panel colour centred on the trigger’s centre (measured against that box) with a radius equal to the distance to the box’s farthest corner. The disc scales from 0 to 1 (a `transform` string, so it runs composited off the main thread) and fades from 0.6 over 750ms on `[0.45, 0.1, 0.2, 1]` (a soft start, so the growth spreads over many frames), while the panel above it is clear: no surface, hairline or shadow. Do not animate a clip-path circle over the panel instead: it repaints the whole panel on every frame and the grow drops to a few stepped frames on a busy machine. When the disc lands, the panel takes back its own surface (identical colour, so nothing changes), its hairline and drop shadow fade in over 300ms, and the frame hides. Content rises in reading order once the disc is nearly whole, at 520ms, 8px with opacity and no blur, 320ms each, staggered 35ms, so nothing shows outside the disc and the last block lands by about 1.05s. Ledger figures count up from zero over 700ms. Close: the content fades out over 100ms while the panel clears, and the same disc scales back into the trigger over 420ms on `[0.4, 0, 1, 1]`. Pending: the label cross-fades to a spinner with the label width locked. Done: the check draws over 340ms, holds 800ms so it can be read, then the dialog closes. Error: the panel shakes horizontally (0, -3, 3, -2, 2, -1, 0) over 340ms the label reads “Couldn’t delete” and the kicker cross-fades (4px rise, 2px blur) to “Couldn’t delete. Nothing was removed.” in the tone ink for 2.6s, so nothing in the layout moves. Reduced motion: no disc, rise or shake; opacity fades of 150ms only, and the figures set immediately.

**Behaviour.** The field receives focus when the dialog opens, or Cancel when typing is off. Delete enables when the trimmed value equals the resource name, and a green check appears at the field’s right edge. Enter in the field runs Delete only when it is enabled. Delete calls `onConfirm` with the name; a returned promise drives pending, done and error, and a throw is an error. `status` and `onStatusChange` make the status controllable for a host that tracks its own mutation; opening straight into a status past idle shows the confirmed name in the field. Only an unmatched name sets the real `disabled` attribute on Delete. While pending and done it stays enabled, focused and at full opacity, with `aria-disabled="true"`, a progress cursor and no press scale, and the field is read-only; on an error focus stays on Delete (or is moved there) for the retry. Archive calls `onArchive` and closes when it settles. Cancel, Esc, a click on the scrim and a finished delete all close the dialog and return focus to the trigger. Focus is trapped with Tab and Shift+Tab, wrapping at both ends. The panel has `tabIndex={-1}`, so a click on its text keeps focus inside and Esc still closes it. The card is `inert` while the dialog is open, the page scroll is locked (padding the body for the vanished scrollbar), and a document `focusin` listener pulls any focus that lands outside the panel back into it. Nothing closes while a delete is pending.

**Accessibility.** The panel is `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="true"`, labelled by its title and described by its body. The trigger has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`. Pending, done and error changes are announced through a polite live region. The spinner and the drawn icons are `aria-hidden`. The focus ring is a 2px outline offset 2px (tone ink on the trigger, the fill on Delete, the theme ink on quiet buttons, and a quieter 40% ink on the field, which shows its ring on open, so it never shouts over the content), shown only on keyboard focus. The dialog title is an h2; the card’s heading level is a prop (default 2). Only truly unavailable controls are 40% opacity with a not-allowed cursor: Delete before the name matches, Cancel and Archive while a delete runs. Every word is a prop (`title`, `description`, `kicker`, `ledgerLabel`, `confirmLabel`, card copy and a `labels` object), with `{name}` standing for the resource name.

**Don’t.** No red triangle or warning icon, no gradient, no glass, no glow, no decorative pill. Don’t let Delete enable from a click alone, don’t close the dialog while a delete is in flight, don’t grey out the confirm while it is working, don’t drop the ledger for a generic “Are you sure?”, and don’t add a second accent colour.
