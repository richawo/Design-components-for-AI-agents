Build a destructive confirmation dialog in React 19 with Tailwind v4 and motion/react. It is a danger-zone card with one trigger button, and pressing it makes a panel grow out of that button and cover the card under a scrim. The visitor must type the exact resource name before the red Delete button enables, so an accidental Enter never deletes anything.

**Layout.** The root is `@container`, at least 640px tall (the panel always fits inside it), centred, and transparent so the page behind shows through; the demo passes a rounded, hairline-framed pane through `className`, so the scrim dims a bounded surface. The card is up to 560px wide, 16px radius, 20px padding (24px from `@md`). Inside it: a mono kicker, a Geist display title, a muted sentence, and the trigger at the end of the row (stacked under the text below `@md`). The panel is absolutely positioned, centred on both axes, `min(460px, 100% - 2rem)` wide, 18px radius, 24px padding (28px from `@md`). The panel content runs top to bottom: kicker “Permanent deletion” (which becomes the error line), title “Delete {name}”, a body sentence, a ledger of what goes (Environments, Last deploy, Environment variables) separated by hairlines, the confirmation field, and a footer. The footer is designed per container width, with Cancel and Delete first in the DOM so Tab goes from the field straight to them. From `@lg` it is one row: “Archive instead” on the left (pulled 12px left so its text aligns with the content edge) and Cancel plus Delete (min-width 168px) on the right. Below `@lg`, Cancel and Delete share a full-width row with Delete filling the rest, and Archive instead sits centred under them. Below `@xs`, Delete stacks over a full-width Cancel. Nothing ever wraps by accident.

**Typography.** Title: display face, 24px, line-height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.03em. Body: 14px, line-height 1.55. Kickers: mono, 10.5px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, muted. Ledger labels 13.5px body colour; figures mono 13.5px with tabular numerals. The confirmation label is 13px and the resource name inside it is mono. The field is mono 14px.

**Colour.** Dark: card #121214, panel #161618, field #0e0e10, hairline rgba(255,255,255,0.08), rule rgba(255,255,255,0.07), ink #f4f4f5, body #b4b4bc, muted #8a8a93, scrim rgba(0,0,0,0.62), success #5cb86f. Light: card and panel #ffffff, field #f6f6f7, hairline rgba(24,24,27,0.10), ink #18181b, body #3f3f46, muted #6b6b74, scrim rgba(18,18,20,0.42), success #2f7d43. The tone sets one fill, one ink and one wash. Danger: fill #d63b40 with white text, ink #ff8b8f on dark (#dc2626, #b91c1c on light). Neutral: fill is the theme ink (#f4f4f5 on dark with #0b0b0c text, #18181b on light with white text). Colour is used only for the destructive action and its trigger; nothing else is tinted. There is no warning triangle.

**Motion.** Open: the panel’s clip-path is a circle that starts at zero radius at the trigger’s centre (measured in the panel’s own coordinates) and grows to 960px over 500ms on `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`; the panel scales from 0.965 to 1 from the same origin and fades from 0.5. Content then rises in reading order, 8px and 4px blur clearing, 420ms each, staggered 50ms from 140ms. Ledger figures count up from zero over 700ms. Close: the same circle shrinks back into the trigger over 260ms on `[0.4, 0, 1, 1]`. Pending: the label cross-fades to a spinner with the label width locked. Done: the check draws over 340ms, holds 520ms, then the dialog closes. Error: the panel shakes horizontally (0, -3, 3, -2, 2, -1, 0) over 340ms the label reads “Couldn’t delete” and the kicker cross-fades (4px rise, 2px blur) to “Couldn’t delete. Nothing was removed.” in the tone ink for 2.6s, so nothing in the layout moves. Reduced motion: no clip, scale, rise or shake; opacity fades of 150ms only, and the figures set immediately.

**Behaviour.** The field receives focus when the dialog opens, or Cancel when typing is off. Delete enables when the trimmed value equals the resource name, and a green check appears at the field’s right edge. Enter in the field runs Delete only when it is enabled. Delete calls `onConfirm` with the name; a returned promise drives pending, done and error, and a throw is an error. Archive calls `onArchive` and closes when it settles. Cancel, Esc, a click on the scrim and a finished delete all close the dialog and return focus to the trigger. Focus is trapped with Tab and Shift+Tab, wrapping at both ends. The card is `inert` while the dialog is open, and the page scroll is locked while open. Nothing closes while a delete is pending.

**Accessibility.** The panel is `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="true"`, labelled by its title and described by its body. The trigger has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`. Pending, done and error changes are announced through a polite live region. The spinner and the drawn icons are `aria-hidden`. The focus ring is a 2px outline offset 2px in the tone ink, shown only on keyboard focus. Disabled controls are 40% opacity with a not-allowed cursor.

**Don’t.** No red triangle or warning icon, no gradient, no glass, no glow, no decorative pill. Don’t let Delete enable from a click alone, don’t close the dialog while a delete is in flight, don’t drop the ledger for a generic “Are you sure?”, and don’t add a second accent colour.
