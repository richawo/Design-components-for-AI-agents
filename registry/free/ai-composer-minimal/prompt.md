Build a minimal AI prompt composer in React 19 with Tailwind v4, motion/react and lucide-free inline SVG icons. It is one focused field that starts as a single line, grows only when the text wraps, and offers @ file mentions and / commands in popovers anchored to the caret.

Layout: a root with @container, max-width 600px, full width below that. The composer is a 16px-radius box, 1px hairline border, fill #141416, inner top highlight and a soft deep shadow, no glow. Inside it a row with 16px left padding, 10px top and bottom, 10px right, and an 8px gap between the field and a 36px square send button (11px radius) aligned to the last line. One line is exactly 36px tall: 22px line box with 7px padding top and bottom. The field grows by 22px per line up to maxRows (default 6), then scrolls inside itself with no visible scrollbar.

Field technique: a textarea with transparent text and an ink caret sits absolutely over a mirror div that lays out the same text with identical font, line-height, tracking, padding and wrapping. The mirror is in flow and therefore sets the field height. Mentions that match a known file are painted in the accent through the mirror, using background tint only, never padding or font weight, so both layers keep identical line breaks. A zero-width space at the end keeps a trailing new line sized.

Popovers: typing @ at the start of a word opens a Files list, and a / at the start of a line opens Commands. The list is 288px wide (never wider than the field), at most 264px tall, with 14px radius, a panel fill #1b1b1e, 1px hairline and a deep shadow. It opens above the whole composer, 8px clear of its top edge, so it never covers text already written; its left edge follows the caret x and is clamped inside the field horizontally. Rows are 2 lines: name in 14px, detail in 11.5px muted. The highlight is one shared layoutId pill that glides on a spring (stiffness 500, damping 40). Up and Down move, Enter or Tab pick, Escape closes and stays closed for that trigger. Picking writes @name plus a trailing space, or /name plus a space.

Typography: field 15px Geist with a 22px line box and -0.005em tracking. Footer 11px Geist Mono, faint colour, with keycaps (5px radius, hairline, chip fill) for return, shift-return and the trigger keys.

Colour: dark ink #f4f4f5, body #c4c4ca, muted #a1a1aa, faint #8a8a94 for placeholder and footer. Send button fill #f4f4f5 with ink #0b0b0c arrow; on hover the fill lifts to #ffffff (light theme: #18181b to #2a2a2e) and the arrow nudges 1px up over 150ms, on pointer devices only. The button keeps a 36px visual with a 4px pseudo-element hit area, so the target is 44px. Press scales to 0.97. The single accent, default #f2b36b, marks only mention text. Light theme: field #ffffff, ink #18181b, accent #a84f24, faint placeholder #6b6b75. A theme prop (dark or light) switches the whole palette and the stage.

Motion: on first view the composer rises 10px out of a 6px blur over 500ms (ease-out), and the footer follows 60ms later; under reduced motion only opacity changes, over 150ms. The popover enters in 240ms (ease-out [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]) from 6px below with a 4px blur clearing, and exits in 120ms. Footer hints swap with a 3px rise. While generating, the send button becomes a stop square inside an inset hairline, and one thin hairline arc (about a third of the circle) draws around it and rotates once every 2.4s. Under reduced motion all movement goes, opacity changes stay at 120ms or less, and the ring is static.

Behaviour: Enter sends the trimmed message and clears the field. Shift+Enter breaks the line. While a menu with options is open, Enter picks the highlighted row instead of sending; an empty menu does not block sending. Send is disabled while empty and idle at 40% opacity. While generating, the button becomes Stop and Escape also stops. Uncontrolled, a send generates for replyMs (1800) or until a returned promise settles. Controlled, the host receives onStateChange and ends the reply.

Accessibility: the field is a textarea labelled "Message" with aria-autocomplete list, aria-controls and aria-activedescendant while a menu is open. Options use role option and aria-selected. The send button has a changing aria-label ("Send message" or "Stop generating"). One polite live region announces "Generating a reply", or while a menu is open its count and highlighted row ("4 files. customer-interview-synthesis.md, 2 of 4", or "No matching files"); the textarea points aria-describedby at a visually hidden hint while a menu is open. Focus stays in the field the whole time, including when a row is pressed (mousedown is prevented). Focus-visible rings are 2px and ink on the button.

Do not: add a "stays on this device" or privacy badge, sparkle icons, a model picker, attachment chips, a gradient ring of light, purple or blue, rounded-full pills everywhere, emoji, or any copy like "Unlock" or "Seamless". The composer is quiet: one field, one button, one menu at a time.
