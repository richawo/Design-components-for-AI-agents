## Free components

Everything in `registry/free/` of the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/richawo/Design-components-for-AI-agents), including code, prompts and JSON prompts, is released under the **MIT licence**. Use it for anything, commercial or not. Attribution is appreciated but not required.

## Pro components

A Pro licence lets you:

- Use every Pro component in **unlimited personal and commercial projects**, including client work and products you sell.
- Modify components however you like.
- Give the code and prompts to AI agents working on your projects, and keep your key in CI and agent environments.

You can't:

- Redistribute, resell or sublicense Pro components, or their prompts, as they are or modified, on their own or as part of another component library, UI kit, template or theme marketplace.
- Share your licence key outside your seat allowance. Pro is for one person; Team covers up to 10.
- Publish Pro source in a public repository. Shipping compiled code in your product is fine, of course.

Lifetime licences include every future Pro component. Yearly licences include everything released while the subscription is active, and you keep the right to use whatever you downloaded during it.
